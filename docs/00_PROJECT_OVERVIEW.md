# MiCatalogo - Project Overview

MiCatalogo is a public product showcase for independent sellers. Visitors discover
products and contact sellers through WhatsApp. The MVP is not a transactional
ecommerce platform: it has no cart, checkout, payments, orders, delivery, or reviews.

## Primary users

- Guests browse, search, filter, and contact sellers.
- Sellers manage one free storefront, its products, categories, media, and metrics.
- Administrators moderate content and manage operational configuration.

## Current phase

Phase 2 - account authentication and security. Phase 0 discovery and Phase 1 bootstrap
are complete under the user-approved Laravel 12, PHP 8.2, and MariaDB 11.4 local stack.
